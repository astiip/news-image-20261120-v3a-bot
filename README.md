## Model: "Front Page" — a classic centred front page, 27 stories

Alternative variant of v2 ("Five Acts") at the user's order: the user found
v2 lacking ("kurang") and asked for another poster option. Same 27 verified
stories as v2 — the most popular / highest-impact ones — with every figure
re-checked by verify.py (27/27 stories, 14/14 DATE CHECK, region bar sums
to 27, no forbidden figures).

Deliberately different composition from v2 and from the leaderboard's
entries: a centred masthead with a giant serif date ("20 NOVEMBER 2026")
and a red italic deck; a six-number strip under the masthead; a region bar
(EUROPE 7 · ASIA 5 · MIDDLE EAST 4 · AMERICAS 4 · OCEANIA 2 · GLOBAL 5 =
27); four big illustrated leads in a 2×2 grid (Kyiv / Cyclone Akvan /
COP31 / GTA VI) with rich, detailed inline SVG scenes drawn in-session;
eight medium cards in a 4-column row, each with its own small SVG banner;
then a dotted world map with numbered pins beside a numbered index for the
other 15 stories; and a dashed DATE CHECK stamp strip for 14 stories from
19 November. No UTC time bands, no right rail, no teal — black ink on
cream with crimson and navy. Legibility first (the judge's recurring
criticism is that dense broadsheet text becomes an "illegible wall" at
~300px): lead decks 10.5px, card decks 10px, index 10px, DATE CHECK 9.5px.
